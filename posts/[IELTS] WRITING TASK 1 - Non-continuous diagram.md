# 1. What is Non-continuous Time Diagrams?
- They are charts, tables, or maps depict the data in two or more separated points of time.
- For example, a bar chart constrasts and compares data in the change of GDP (Gross Domestic Property) in a country over a 6-year period, from 2000 to 2006.
